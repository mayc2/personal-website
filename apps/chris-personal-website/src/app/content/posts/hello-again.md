This site sat untouched from August 2021 until this week. The old version was a dark single page with a carousel of project names, a Node 16 toolchain, and a welcome modal that told you it was a work in progress. It was.

The new one is built the way I'd build anything today: an Nx workspace, Vite, React 19, and a small component library on top of shadcn and Radix that I own in the repo. The palette is warm and light on purpose. Most of what I read online is dark mode and high contrast, and I wanted the opposite: paper, sage, a little peach, and type that gets out of the way.

I also wanted somewhere to write. Most of my work the last few years has been on agent systems for clinical research: provenance for long-running analyses, versioned agent state, evaluations that catch regressions before a researcher does. Those problems don't fit in a tweet and I keep re-explaining them in Slack. So they'll end up here instead.

No schedule, no newsletter. If something here is useful or wrong, [email me](mailto:r16cmay@gmail.com).
